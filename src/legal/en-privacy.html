Privacy policy

Effective: [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

This policy explains what data the Lawha app for business owners and the Lawha Player app on screens collect, and how we use and protect it. We follow Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law. If the Arabic and English versions differ, the Arabic version applies.

Who we are

[[COMPANY_NAME]], [[COMPANY_ADDRESS]]. For anything about your data: contact@lawha.tech.

What we collect

  • Account: your name and email; your phone number if you sign in with it; or your Google or Apple account if you choose that. Passwords are stored encrypted by the sign-in service and we never see them.
  • Business: the business name and logo, and its branches and their time zones.
  • Content: the photos and videos you upload, and the playlists, schedules and announcements you create.
  • Team: your team members' names, emails and roles, and the invites you send.
  • Screens: an anonymous ID for each device, whether it is online, its app version, free storage, and how many times each item played. The player collects no personal data.
  • Notifications: your phone's push token, so we can tell you when a screen goes offline.
  • Crash and usage reports: app crash reports, and four events only: account created, screen paired, media uploaded, playlist assigned. They contain no names or emails.

The camera is used only to scan a screen's code, and nothing it sees is saved. We only access the photos and videos you pick.

How we use it

  • To run the service: show your content on your screens, keep your team in sync, and send screen alerts.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To fix crashes and see where people get stuck.

We show no ads, sell no data, don't track you across other apps or websites, and don't use the advertising ID.

Where it is stored

We use Google Firebase. Your data is stored outside Saudi Arabia:

  • Content, business, team and screen data, sign-in (email and phone number) and screen online status: Google's data centers in the United States.
  • Notifications, crash and usage reports: Google's global services.

Who we share it with

  • Your team: members see business data according to their role.
  • Google: as a service provider processing data on our behalf.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Account data and content: until you delete them or your account, or 90 days after a subscription ends without renewal. We remind you a week before.
  • Play counts from screens: 90 days.
  • Screen pairing codes: 10 minutes.
  • Crash and usage reports: up to 90 days for crashes, two months for usage events.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and the business you own, with all its content, branches and playlists, and unpairs its screens. Your team members keep their own logins but lose access to your business. You can also ask for deletion by email.

Your rights

You can see your data, correct it, ask for a copy or for deletion, and object to its processing. Email contact@lawha.tech and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Only a business's members can reach its data, according to their role, and each device can read only its own screen's data.

Children

The service is for business owners and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
